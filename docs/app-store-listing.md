## Full Description (4000 chars max)

**Stop losing receipts. Start tracking expenses effortlessly.**

Zenvoy turns your phone into a powerful receipt scanner and expense tracker. Just point your camera at any receipt — Zenvoy's AI instantly extracts the vendor, date, total, and category so you never have to type anything manually.

### What Zenvoy Does

**Scan Any Receipt**
Capture receipts with your camera or import from your photo library. Our AI (powered by Claude) reads the receipt and fills in all the details automatically — even for handwritten receipts and non-English text.

**Stay Organized**
Receipts are automatically sorted into categories: Food & Drink, Transport, Shopping, Business, Healthcare, Entertainment. Your Inbox shows items that need a quick review before they're filed away.

**Track Your Spending**
The Analytics screen shows your spending breakdown by category with bar and pie charts. Set a monthly budget and get notified when you're approaching your limit.

**Export Instantly**
Generate PDF or CSV reports with one tap. Share expense reports with your accountant, employer, or import into your spreadsheet of choice.

**Search & Filter**
Find any receipt in seconds with full-text search. Filter by date range, category, or amount.

**Sync Across Devices** *(Pro)*
Enable cloud sync to access your receipts on any device. Zenvoy uses Firebase for secure, private cloud storage — only you can see your data.

### Privacy First
Your receipt data belongs to you. All data is stored locally on your device by default. Cloud sync is opt-in. We never sell your data.
